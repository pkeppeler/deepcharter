# Every custom key the mod registers (#1556), one per line: `key.deepcharter.<id>  <why no vanilla key or click fits>`.
# checkKeybindGate fails on a key not listed here, and on an entry no key uses. Read the reasons: the gate counts words, not truth.
key.deepcharter.handbook  opens the handbook from the world with no item held or screen open; vanilla has no key for a mod's book, and its advancements key (L) is rerouted to the handbook as well (ADR 0067)
key.deepcharter.catalogue.recipes  copies EMI's R on a hovered item, which recipe-viewer players already know; vanilla has no item-lookup key and a click on a slot picks the item up
key.deepcharter.catalogue.uses  copies EMI's U, the pair of R, for what a hovered item is used in; a click on a slot already picks the item up
key.deepcharter.catalogue.favourite  copies EMI's A to star a hovered item; a click on a slot already picks the item up or moves it
key.deepcharter.catalogue.spec_sheet  opens the hovered item's spec sheet, or the latest offered with none hovered; vanilla has no such key, a click already opens recipes (EMI's rule), and the offer has no on-screen target
key.deepcharter.catalogue.toggle  shows or hides the catalogue panels on any inventory screen; no vanilla key does this and the panels leave no room for a button on every screen
key.deepcharter.catalogue.back  copies EMI's Backspace to return to the recipe shown before; vanilla has no history on a panel and a click would need a button on a cramped panel
key.deepcharter.catalogue.materials  switches the recipe console between the recipe and its total materials while the pointer is on the recipe; no vanilla key applies
key.deepcharter.line_slab  the pilot lines the slab or stops lining, in a pod with no screen open; vanilla has no action for it and every click on a pod is already spent
key.deepcharter.sonar_ping  the navigator pings the sonar on demand; vanilla has no sonar action and the clicks aboard belong to piloting
key.deepcharter.sonar_mark  marks or clears the echo being looked at, beside the ping key; vanilla has no equivalent and the clicks aboard belong to piloting
