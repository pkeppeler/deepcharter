# The time budget of CI (#1574, ADR 0115): no job runs longer than job-limit-minutes, and each shard of a suite holds at most the seconds
# below of tests by weight, so that the job fits the limit on a runner slow-runner-factor times slower than the ones measured.
# tools/tests/test_ci_budget.py enforces all of it: `timeout-minutes` on every job, no class heavier than its shard budget, and the
# shard counts in the workflows (CLIENT_SHARDS, BUILD_SHARDS) enough for the weights. Lines are `<name> <number>`.
#
# One job is: setup (the job's start, the blobless checkout, the pinned merge of main, the compiled build's download and the Gradle cache
# restore) + the program's start-up + the shard's tests. Measured on PR #1730's two full runs (68 client and 32 server shard jobs, #1724):
# a client shard's first test at p50 61 s, p90 75 s from the job's start (13 s of checkout and download, 5 s of Gradle cache, 3 s of
# assets, 14 s of Gradle's first call, 5 s of the run task's start, 17 s of Minecraft); a server shard's first test batch at p50 49 s,
# p90 59 s. Before #1724 they were p50 89 s and 78 s: every shard rescanned the gametest sources and downloaded 288 POMs while configuring.
# A slow runner is 1.3 times slower in all of it, and the worst job must stay under `worst-job-seconds` (280 s, 20 s under the limit)
# there: (65 + 127) * 1.3 = 250 s for a client shard.
job-limit-minutes 5
slow-runner-factor 1.3
# The most any job may take at that factor, under the limit of 300 s.
worst-job-seconds 280
# The setup a client shard spends before its first test, and a server shard's (seconds, measured above): at least the p50, and at the
# slow-runner factor at least the p90 (65 * 1.3 = 85 >= 75; 50 * 1.3 = 65 >= 59).
client-overhead-seconds 65
server-overhead-seconds 50
# Seconds from the job's start at which the client action ends its run with a message (.github/actions/client-gametest), 30 s under the limit
# for the post steps and the runner's teardown. The deadline counts from the job's start, so slow setup shortens the tests' time, not the message.
client-deadline-seconds 270
# Seconds of client tests (gradle/client-test-weights.txt) one client shard holds: (270 - 20) / 1.3 - 65, rounded down, so the slowest
# modelled shard on a slow runner ends 20 s before the client deadline (the margin #1695 found missing).
client-shard-seconds 127
# Seconds of server GameTests (gradle/server-test-weights.txt) one build shard holds. Measured on the first sharded run of #1574 (13 shards
# of 115 s): 114 to 178 s of tests, 1.0 to 1.5 times its weight, because each shard also starts the campaign environments its classes
# use. With the setup above: (50 + 1.55 * 105) * 1.3 = 277 s.
server-shard-seconds 105
# Seconds of tool tests (tools/tests/ci-tool-test-weights.txt) one tool-test part holds, after about 40 s of setup: (40 + 100) * 1.3 = 182 s.
tool-part-seconds 100
# A gate-check job (tools/tests/gate_checks.py, one Gradle run over fixture trees) costs, at the measured speed: the job's setup and
# Gradle's start-up, one scan of the real tree (the scan is cached per tree inside a run, so every case that shards the real tree shares it),
# and a small scan per fixture tree. Measured on run 46126845034 (b3a2ae36), before the cache: the 480 fixture trees of the last
# job took 45 s, and the client_filter job's 11 real-tree cases took 214 s (about 19 s each), server_shard's 17 over 280 s.
# tools/tests/test_tool_test_parts.py prices each job from its cases and keeps it under worst-job-seconds at the slow-runner factor.
gate-setup-seconds 85
gate-real-tree-scan-seconds 20
gate-fixture-case-seconds 0.2
