# The time budget of CI (#1574, ADR 0115): no job runs longer than job-limit-minutes, and each shard of a suite holds at most the seconds
# below of tests by weight, so that the job fits the limit on a runner slow-runner-factor times slower than the ones measured.
# tools/tests/test_ci_budget.py enforces all of it: `timeout-minutes` on every job, no class heavier than its shard budget, and the
# shard counts in the workflows (CLIENT_SHARDS, BUILD_SHARDS) enough for the weights. Lines are `<name> <number>`.
#
# One job is: setup (the job's start, the blobless checkout, the merge of main, the compiled build's download and the Gradle cache
# restore, about 65 s measured on the client shards) + the program's start-up (Minecraft's, about 35 s) + the shard's tests. Both
# suites run 1.3 times slower on a slow runner, so (65 + 35 + budget) * 1.3 stays under 300 s: 125 s of client tests is 292 s.
job-limit-minutes 5
slow-runner-factor 1.3
# Seconds of client tests (gradle/client-test-weights.txt) one client shard holds.
client-shard-seconds 125
# Seconds of server GameTests (gradle/server-test-weights.txt) one build shard holds. Measured on the first sharded run of this change
# (13 shards of 115 s): 59 s of setup and start-up, and 114 to 178 s of tests, so a shard took 173 to 237 s, 1.0 to 1.5 times its weight
# in tests, because each shard also starts the campaign environments its classes use. At 100 s the slowest should take about 215 s:
# (59 + 1.55 * 100) * 1.3 = 280 s.
server-shard-seconds 100
# Seconds of tool tests (tools/tests/ci-tool-test-weights.txt) one tool-test part holds, after about 40 s of setup: (40 + 100) * 1.3 = 182 s.
tool-part-seconds 100
