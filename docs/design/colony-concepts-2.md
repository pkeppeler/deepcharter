# Colony concepts, round 2

Issue [#353](https://github.com/pkeppeler/deepcharter/issues/353), PR [#357](https://github.com/pkeppeler/deepcharter/pull/357). Round 1 is [colony-concepts.md](colony-concepts.md).

You asked for The Works' style with Boomtown Mill's mining detail (its signs, and the black iron headframe with its trussed conveyor), nothing western, at about half the size, at the scale of a player and a pod, not a city. And for statue C, the Host, at four sizes. Here are four ways to lay out the ore house in that style, and the Host at 20, 15, 10 and 6 blocks.

Everything is built in the game from the colony kit and shot in a test world. The close stills have a pod and a player in them for scale. From far away and from the air they are too small to make out. The pod is the Mole: about 2 blocks wide, high and long. It is still the placeholder cube until you pick a Mole design. Nothing in a player's world changes until you pick.

## How to pick

Pick two things. They are separate: any statue size goes with any layout.

1. **A layout: A, B, C or D.** The four use the same style and the same parts, and differ in how the ore house and the headframe sit round the square.
2. **A statue size: 20, 15, 10 or 6 blocks.** The layouts show the Host at 10.

You can mix parts too, for example "B, but with D's gantry". Reply on the PR, for example "C, statue 10".

| | Round 1 (The Works) | Round 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Ore house | 25 x 17 blocks, walls 13 high, its roof machinery to 24 | 14 x 11 blocks (A and D), walls 8 high, its roof machinery to 14 to 16 |

A player is 1.8 blocks tall. The Mole pod fits the bay with room to spare, and the bigger Prospector pod (about 3 blocks) fits it too.

## Shared by all four

- **The style of The Works:** dark riveted plate in a grid of red pilasters, a brass band at the upper floor, lit windows along the upper floor, glowing crusher hatches, gauge panels, hazard-striped parapets, railed roofs, and a sodium lamp on each pilaster.
- **Boomtown's headframe, at half height:** black lattice, X-braced, a railed deck at 22 with two wheels, a gantry and a beacon at 30, back legs to the hoist house, ropes down the shaft to two skips. A riveted tipple bin hangs on its side, and a black trussed conveyor runs from it to the ore house.
- **Mining at a player's size:** ore cars on mine track at every bay, roller shutters, steel ladders up the walls, ore bins on legs, and stacks.
- **Boomtown's signs:** H. COLOM & CO., ORE HOUSE, SHAFT NO 1 on the headframe, the company name on the headframe deck, DEEPER TOGETHER! lit on the hoist house, OUR FOUNDER on the plinth. ORE HOUSE, SHAFT NO 1 and OUR FOUNDER are still placeholders for the lore pass.
- **Not western:** no cream corrugated gables, no red barn roofs, no wooden false fronts.

## A. Pithead Works

The compact one. One two-storey hall stands right beside the headframe, on the north-east corner of the square. The tipple bin's conveyor drops a few blocks into a crusher house on the hall's roof. Two ore bins on legs and a stack stand behind it. Everything is in one tight group, so the square stays open.

- Hall 14 x 11, 10 high to the roof railing; crusher house to 14; ore bins to 15; stack 22.

## B. Gallery Works

The spread-out one. A crusher house stands at the foot of the headframe, under the tipple bin. From its roof a long trussed conveyor climbs on two lattice bents to a bin house over the west end of a long, low, one-storey hall set back from the square. The bay is under a grating canopy on red legs. The long diagonal conveyor is the thing you see first.

- Crusher house 6 x 8, 7 high; hall 19 x 10, 8 high to its railing; bin house to 16; conveyor 15 blocks long, climbing 6; stack 20.

## C. Terrace Works

The gravity mill, cut into a mesa edge west of the square. Ore goes uphill once and then falls: the conveyor from the tipple bin climbs to three ore bins on the mesa top, a crusher house stands on a bench below them, and the hall where the pods load is at the foot, each a storey lower than the last. The mesa is regolith rock in beds of ochre, faced in concrete where the buildings meet it.

- Mesa 10 high; ore bins and their catwalk to 22; crusher house on the bench to 14; hall 8 x 13, 10 high to its railing; stack 26.

## D. Gantry Works

The ore house faces the headframe across the square, and a trussed conveyor gantry joins them over the square. It runs from the tipple bin to a lattice transfer tower at the square's corner, then straight across the square at 16 on one bent, into a head house on the hall's roof. Pods fly under it. The square becomes a yard closed on three sides: the headframe, the gantry and the ore house.

- Hall 14 x 11, 10 high to its railing; head house to 16; transfer tower to 19; the gantry spans 24 blocks at 16; stack 22.

## The Host at four sizes

The same statue (statue C of round 1: mid-stride, one open hand raised beside his head in welcome, the other lowered and turned out, empty, with the slot in his chest), in layout A's square, from the same five views at every size, with a pod and a player at the foot of the plinth in the close views (from the air they are too small to make out). The plinth grows with him.

| Size | Plinth | Plinth and statue | Times a player |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20 blocks (round 1's) | 10 | 30 | 11 |
| 15 blocks | 7 | 22 | 8 |
| 10 blocks (in the layouts) | 5 | 15 | 5.5 |
| 6 blocks | 3 | 9 | 3.3 |

At 20 he is as tall as the headframe and taller than every building, so the square reads as his. At 10 he stands about level with the ore house's roof machinery. At 6 the headframe and the stacks are the landmarks, and he is a monument you walk up to.

![The Host at four sizes](https://github.com/pkeppeler/deepcharter/blob/pr-media/357/statues-side-by-side.png?raw=true)

None of the four sizes reads as a cross. A test draws his outline from 18 views round and above him, and at each size from 48 places in the square at a player's eye height. It fails when both arms stick out past the body in one row of the upper body. His worst score is 1.45 (at 20 blocks); the test fails at 2, and a figure with its arms straight out scores over 3. It is an outline check, so the stills are the proof.

## What comes later

- **The rebuild ([#244](https://github.com/pkeppeler/deepcharter/issues/244))** builds the whole town in the layout you pick: the pay office, the bunkhouse, the chapel and the rest, at this scale.
- **The kit** now holds only what these four use. The western and Moderne parts of round 1 (corrugated steel, the gabled roofs, the cream enamel, the red steel frame, the mill windows, the red lattice) and the other two statues are gone. New: roller shutters, steel ladders, mine track, ore cars and the trussed conveyor.
- **Not in the concepts:** motion (the wheels, belts and cars do not move yet), smoke, and the other buildings.
