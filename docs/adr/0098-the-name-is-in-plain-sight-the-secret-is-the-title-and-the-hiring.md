# The name is in plain sight; the secret is the title and the hiring

A playtester decoded H. COLOM as MOLOCH in two minutes, so the hidden-name trick is retired (pkeppeler, 2026-11-25, #1335). The name is dramatic irony: the player knows, and the characters act as if nobody does. The secret the story protects is that "H. Colom" is a title worn by a former Director, and that it is hiring: the campaign is a recruitment drive.

The layer-1 margin note stays as a wink, not a puzzle. The layer-5 echo and the layer-8 letter slide stay, as the game dropping the pretence. Roz's T41 now points at the masks and the open seat instead of explaining the name. [LORE.md section 4](../LORE.md#4-the-employer-h-colom--co).

- **Harder clues**: rejected. A cipher this short falls to any sharp player, and a story that depends on it breaks when it does.
