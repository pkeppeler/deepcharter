# Renunciation needs the whole online crew; signing stays the Director's

Amends [ADR 0031](0031-the-finale-is-won-by-renunciation.md). Until now any member holding the Controlling Interest could throw it into the Furnace alone and burn the whole charter's account. The user decided (pkeppeler, 2026-11-25, #1335) that renouncing is done together: the holder holds the deed out at the fire's edge, and the fire takes it only when every **online** charter member stands at the edge and lets go too, each by their own action in the world (no vote screen). Anyone stepping back cancels it, and nothing burns until the last hand lets go. The finale build chooses the exact interaction.

Signing is unchanged: the Director's alone. Evil is a private deal; renouncing is done together. A Director who signs over the crew is a betrayal story, not a wipe. The payout and dividend go to the charter's shared account (each charter has one), so the crew lives off the Retained's labour too.

## Considered Options

- **Count every member, online or not**: rejected. A member who quit the game could block the true ending forever.
- **A vote screen**: rejected. Letting go is a deed in the world, like the rest of the finale.
- **Keep one thrower**: rejected. It let one player burn everyone's account.

## Consequences

- Offline members do not count. Accepted trade-off: a group could wait until someone logs off.
- The signing payout is shared, so a signed charter's crew is paid, but only the Director becomes the Chairman.
