# Each charter member crafts one Mole, and a wreck keeps its owner's place

Amends [ADR 0021](0021-the-hangar-is-a-terminal-and-its-repair-founds-the-mole.md): the hangar's `buy_mole` action, the refurbished Mole and its prices are retired. [ADR 0028](0028-chapters-one-to-five-complete-from-the-events-that-own-the-deed.md) credits "Repair the Mole" to the new `assemble_mole` action instead. User decisions of 2026-11-27 on issue #1595.

## Decision

- **A place per player.** Each player has one place for a crafted Mole. The hangar's saved data (`HangarData`) holds each place: the player, the Mole that holds it, whether that Mole is a wreck, and a filed write-off's due time. The player's own UUID names it, not the charter's: a charter of three members can have three crafted Moles, and a member who changes charter keeps their place.
- **Crafting is assembling at the hangar console.** `assemble_mole` takes the four hangar parts (tread assembly, rotor hub, drill head, fuel injector, the crafted bootstrap-tier items that already repair the founding Mole) from the player's pack and stands a Mole in a free place of the bay, registered to the player's charter. It costs no money and no catalyst, and a refused assembly takes nothing. The action takes no arguments, as before.
- **The founding Mole is its founder's one Mole.** The hangar gives the founding Mole's place to the player who put in the last part. A world saved before this change has no such record: the founding Mole gives its place to the founding charter's Director when it loads (`Hangar.onLoad`). Other Moles of such a world (a refurbished purchase, a restored wreck) hold no place, so the rule counts only what is crafted after it. A world is never asked to take a Mole away.
- **A wreck keeps the place, and only the wreck's end frees it.** A Mole holds its place, working or wrecked, until it is scrapped or written off. Restoring a wreck, at the hangar or later in the field, makes the same Mole work and the place stays held. The hangar listens to `WreckEvents.WRECKED` and `WreckEvents.RESTORED` (new, fired by `Wrecks`) to keep each place's wreck flag, so a write-off can be checked without loading the pod. The field kit (#1608) restores through `Wrecks.restore` and needs no change here, and the rescue tug (#1627) tows a wreck home without taking a place.
- **Scrapping needs the wreck at the console.** `scrap_wreck` scraps the nearest wreck in reach that the charter owns, for no money. The hold's contents go to the player's pack (what does not fit drops), the fitted parts drop as items, and the pod is removed. There is no remote scrapping, so money cannot buy a way out of a rescue. A wreck nobody owns is restored first, not scrapped, so the lore Prospector cannot be lost.
- **The wreck keeps what was in it.** The fitted upgrades are in the pod's component attachment, and the hold's ore in its cargo, and a wreck is never emptied or removed on its own. When the hull runs out, what each crew member carries moves into the wreck (`WreckPack`, a saved attachment that is not synced, each stack under the UUID of the player who carried it) and the crew die with empty hands, so nothing drops to despawn, burn or be taken; the handbook stays with its player. The pack goes back only to the player who carried it: salvaging the wreck gives you your own stacks and the hold, never another player's. A restore or scrap gives each stack to its pilot if they are online and alive, else to the Mole's owner if they are, and a restore or scrap waits (`pack_owner_away`) while neither is, so the pack stays in the wreck until one of them is online; so a charter-mate cannot restore or scrap a member's wreck and keep that member's pack. The hold is anyone's to salvage, as before. No decay is added: a wreck stays until someone restores, scraps or writes it off.
- **Write-off, the last resort.** `write_off` is pressed twice: the first press arms it and the console's screen shows the terms and a confirm button for 30 seconds (the action bar says only that it is armed), and the second press within that time files it. It files a write-off of the player's own Mole once it is recorded as a wreck, wherever it lies: it costs `HangarTuning.writeOffCost` ($2,000, more than a Prospector's restore money, and a catalyst is not part of it) from the charter's account and takes `writeOffTicks` (24,000, one in-game day, which is 20 minutes of real time; every line about it says minutes; counted in game time so sleeping does not shorten it). When it is due the place is free and the wreck is destroyed with its parts and cargo, with no drop. A wreck whose chunk is not loaded then is condemned in the data and destroyed when it loads. A Mole that still works cannot be written off, so no one writes off a stuck pod to skip the way home. Restoring or scrapping a wreck before the day is out cancels the filing and refunds its price to the charter that paid. The place is freed after the wreck is destroyed (or condemned), never before, so a step that fails leaves the place held and the write-off due again.
- **Scrap is the owner's.** A wreck that holds a place is scrapped by the holder, or by the owning charter's Director as a fallback. The caller's own wreck is chosen first among those in reach, and only registered wrecks the charter may use count, so a nearer wreck nobody owns never blocks it.
- **Fees.** The growing registration fee only ever sat on the refurbished Mole, and that purchase is retired with it. Wreck chassis keep their restore prices in `HangarTuning.restoreCosts` (money, and catalysts for every chassis after the Mole: restoring a Mole wreck takes $100 and no catalyst). The Mole is exempt from the catalyst rule: SPEC section 4 now says "every chassis after the Mole needs a rare material" (a user-approved SPEC edit).
- **Saved state.** `HangarData` is version 2 and still reads version 1: the `moles` and `condemned` fields read as empty from it, and `legacy_founding_place` reads as true, which gives the founding Mole its place once when it loads. A test loads the previous form, including a wrecked founding Mole and a Director who holds a place already. A place is also freed when its pod is removed as killed or discarded (not unloaded or moved across a seam), and `/deepcharter hangar release <player>` frees one by hand for an operator.

## Considered Options

- **A place per charter.** Rejected by the user: a friend who joins should start with a pod of their own.
- **Scrap or write off from a distance without a wait.** Rejected by the user: a player stuck deep could buy a spare and skip the rescue.
- **Wreck decay over time.** Declined by the user.
- **A pod item crafted in the grid and placed in the world.** Rejected: a refusal for a second Mole cannot be shown at a crafting grid, and the console already has the refusal, the bay and the sound.
- **Reading the place from the pods in the world.** Rejected: the world keeps no list of pods, and a Mole in an unloaded chunk would look absent (the rule against gating on entity queries).

## Consequences

- A solo player who is broke and whose Mole is a wreck they cannot tow has no free way out: the place stays held until they earn $2,000 on foot or a crewmate tows the wreck home. That is the fuel clock working ([ADR 0104](0104-a-stranded-pod-is-the-players-to-get-home-and-the-company-sends-no-crew.md)), not a gap to patch. The rescue tug (#1627) and the field kit (#1608) are the later counterplay.
- A pod removed as killed or discarded frees its place, and the operator command frees any other.
- The write-off wait and cost, and the $0 price of a crafted Mole, are invented numbers in `HangarTuning`.
- The handbook and the T06 transmission fire on assembling a Mole as they did on buying one.
