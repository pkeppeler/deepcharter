# The finale is won by renunciation

The final fight breaks the boss but cannot kill him. It ends in a choice, and only giving everything up wins. The boss offers the charter a Controlling Interest in the employer. If anyone in the charter throws it into the Furnace (renunciation), the boss dies, the charter loses its whole account, and every crew member gets an Unnumbered Lamp. If the Director signs it, the decision is final: he becomes the Chairman with a payout, a dividend and a suit lamp that never works again, and the epilogue is grim.

We chose this because the user wants the story's darkness in the manner of Tolkien and Lewis: evil is hollow and finite, and it is beaten by refusing it, not by firepower alone. The wrong choice has to be genuinely tempting, or the choice is inert, so signing pays well and costs in ways that embody the theme. Lore: [LORE.md section 12](../LORE.md#12-the-finale).

## Considered Options

- **The fight kills the boss**, as in the original: rejected. It makes the victory pure firepower.
- **Keep the money on renunciation**: rejected by the user. Renouncing has to cost everything.
- **Let a signed charter repent later**: rejected by the user. Signing is the end of that charter's story.
- **Wipe pods and components too, like the original's New Game+**: rejected. It would erase 60 hours of co-op work.

## Consequences

- Story state is per charter, and so is every change after the finale.
- Only members of the charter that broke Head Office can hold its Controlling Interest. It cannot leave the Boardroom, so the choice is made there.
- Renouncing is the one story event that changes a charter's economy (its account goes to zero); it changes no rules. Serial numbers burn off as presentation only, and components stay bound to the charter.
- The Unnumbered Lamp belongs to the player. The Controlling Interest and the dead suit lamp follow the Director even if he leaves the charter.
