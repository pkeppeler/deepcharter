# Deep Charter: lore bible

Canon, settled 2026-10-07 in the lore session ([#12](https://github.com/pkeppeler/deepcharter/issues/12)).

**This document is full of spoilers and is for the team only.** None of it is player-facing unless it is marked as such. Player-facing text lives in:

- [lore/transmissions.md](lore/transmissions.md): the transmission script and its triggers
- [lore/handbook.md](lore/handbook.md): the Employee Handbook's voice, margin notes and revisions
- [lore/notes.md](lore/notes.md): the starter set of Notes

Glossary terms are defined in [CONTEXT.md](../CONTEXT.md); the game's systems are in [SPEC.md](SPEC.md). The finale's design rationale is in [ADR 0006](adr/0006-the-finale-is-won-by-renunciation.md).

## 1. One-line pitch (internal)

H. Colom & Company has reopened Prosperity, a mining colony that emptied in one night 38 years ago. It pays well and buys everything you dig. Its Chairman's name, read backwards, is MOLOCH.

## 2. Tone

**Layered by depth.** Each act has one dominant register, so a joke never lands on a dread beat.

| Act | Layers | Register |
|-----|--------|----------|
| 1 | 1–2 | Satire. The Company as Screwtape would run it: cheerful, efficient, ridiculous. |
| 2 | 3–5 | Dread. The dead, the wrecks, the growing sense that the deep is alive. |
| 3 | 6–7 | The idol unmasked. The Chairman's politeness curdles. |
| Finale | 8 | Pompous villainy that breaks into holy terror. |

**Christian in shape, never in label.** Darkness here is the kind Tolkien and Lewis wrote:

- Evil is real, but it can't create; it can only counterfeit and consume.
- Evil is hollow, finite and beaten.
- The deepest thing in the world is good, and it is not safe.
- Victory comes through renunciation, not firepower alone.

### Guardrails

1. The Company and its Chairman get the jokes. The dead never do.
2. **The lure never goes dark.** No story beat ever blocks selling, buying or upgrading. The Chairman's threats come with a receipt. This protects pillar 1.
3. The handbook can change its tone, never its rules.
4. Faith is quiet and unnamed. No denomination, no quoted scripture, no sermons. It shows in the miners' private lives: a candle, a hymn, a prayer card.
5. The thing at the bottom is never named, gendered or explained in game text. The game hints; it never explains.
6. **No children in the deep.** In scripture, Moloch is the idol children were sacrificed to. We keep his shape and his hunger and nothing else: no child characters, victims or sacrifice imagery. Miners may mention their families.
7. **No gore** (the default; the user may lift it). Horror comes from wrongness and absence: an empty cab, a loop counter, a punch clock still ticking.
8. Every story change after the finale is presentation for that charter only, never a world edit.
9. Homage, not copy. Beats may echo the original game (a retiring miner, an altimeter's rating, a strike that goes wrong), but no line or distinctive phrase is reused. No Natas, no Mars.

## 3. The spine

You sign with H. Colom & Company to reopen Prosperity, a company town that emptied in a single night. The Company pays bonuses, issues a cheerful handbook and buys every ore you haul. Nothing is refined "offsite." The colony's ore processor feeds the **Conduit**, a Company pipe that runs straight down through every layer to the Furnace, which the Company calls **Head Office**.

The deep is the body of a vast creature, made and good, asleep (the **Sleeper**). Ore is what it grows, which is why ore gets richer the closer you get to its heart. In its heart cavity sits **Moloch**: an ancient parasite in the shape of an idol, hollow and twisted. It cannot make anything; it only takes. It cannot dig, so it needs hands, and it grows lures to get them: light, welcome, wages, the promise of retirement, every one a counterfeit of a good thing. The Company is its largest lure. The Chairman is a man wearing its mask.

You fight your way to the Furnace and break its body, but that does not kill it. It offers you everything. Signing makes your Director its next mask. Throwing the deed back into the fire, and every coin you own with it, is the only thing that kills it. Then the floor opens, and the thing you have been digging into all game opens its eye.

## 4. The employer: H. Colom & Co.

**Dress**

- Name: *H. Colom & Co. — Extraction · Refining · Personnel.*
- Logo: a bull's head whose horns are two crossed picks.
- Seal motto: *NIHIL REDDITUR* ("nothing is returned"), presented as a no-refunds policy.
- Slogan: *Deeper Together!*

**The name.** H. COLOM reversed, letter by letter, is MOLOCH. The initial is part of the trick: "nobody knows what the H stands for," because it stands for nothing. It is the last letter of his name. The clue ladder:

| Where | Clue | Channel |
|-------|------|---------|
| Layer 1 (the handbook, from hour one) | Beside the Founder's letter: *"Twenty years here and nobody knows what the H stands for. —R."* | Margin note |
| Layer 5 | Every Company transmission echoes with its sender reversed: `H. COLOM & CO. ↩ .OC & MOLOC .H` | Transmission header |
| Layer 7 | Roz: *"It isn't an initial, you know. It's the end of his name."* | Transmission T41 |
| Layer 8 | The sender field's letters slide into MOLOCH while he speaks. | Transmission T50 |

**Moloch.** A parasite older than any colony, fastened to the Sleeper's hide. Over ages it burrowed toward the heart, leaving cold furnaces behind it (the Ossuary). It now sits in the heart cavity, but it has never broken the wall around the heart (the Ramp). Its body is the Furnace and everything built in it. At the centre of Head Office stands a mountain-sized bronze figure with open hands and a furnace mouth in its chest, built from every delivery ever made.

**The Chairman.** "H. Colom" is a title: a smiling bronze mask worn by whoever last signed. Every Chairman was once a charter's Director who took the deed at the bottom and was used up until nobody was left inside. The current one is **Augustin Fell**, commander of Survey One, who signed in CY 0. After 61 years he is nearly spent.

- **Why the Company is hiring now:** the mask needs a new face. The whole campaign is, without anyone saying so, a recruitment drive for the Chairmanship.
- **Two wills.** Act 3's "turn back" threats are Fell, clinging to his seat. The finale's offer is Moloch, who wants a fresh mask. The Company's own checkpoint waves you through while its Chairman orders you home.

**In plain sight.** The Founder statue in the colony square is bronze, with arms out, palms up and a slot in its chest labelled SUGGESTIONS. It has the same silhouette as the figure at the centre of Head Office. An early work order asks the charter to deliver Bronzium to "restore the Founder's hands."

## 5. What's at the bottom: the Sleeper

"The Sleeper" is a name for this document only; the game never names it, and never calls it "she" or "mother." It is a vast creature, made and good, asleep, written in the register of Job's Leviathan: good, not safe. Next to it, the eight layers are a scratch in its hide.

| Layer | Hidden reading |
|-------|----------------|
| 1 The Claim | skin |
| 2 The Old Workings | scar tissue: the colony's own wounds |
| 3 Fungal Hollows | what grows on it |
| 4 The Drowned Deep | fluid |
| 5 The Lattice | nerve: it listens, and it remembers every voice |
| 6 The Magma Belt | blood: the lava pulses downward on a slow beat |
| 7 The Ossuary | bone: its ribs, Moloch's old feeding sites, its eyes |
| 8 The Furnace | the heart cavity, with the parasite in it |
| Ramp | the wall around the heart |
| Uncharted | deeper strata that repeat the layers above, warped, as if it dreams them; nameless things gnaw there |

What it explains:

- Ore value rises with depth.
- Earthquakes are its pulse, heard through the Company's noise.
- The Ramp is why Moloch never reached the heart.
- "The depths moved" (the splice event) means it stirred in its sleep.

**The Motherload** is the miners' word for the strike of a lifetime. It is the heart. The game never explains it further.

**THE EYES** in layer 7 are its eyes, set where no eyes belong. They never attack. Being watched by them is worse.

**Its defences.** Some of the deep's creatures treat drills as an infection. They are dangerous to you because you work for the parasite.

## 6. Motifs

- **Lamps.** A lamp is life, and quietly, faith.
  - The Retained are lampless. "Things come to dark pods."
  - The colony chapel's candle has burned for 38 years with nobody tending it. Nobody ever comments.
  - Ines left the Prospector's lights on for her brother; one is still burning.
  - Convoy Nine's lamps are still lit under the water.
  - Moloch's glow is counterfeit light.
  - Renunciation gives every crew member a lamp that never goes out. Signing puts the Director's out forever.
- **Hands.** The Founder's open bronze hands hold nothing. The Company needs your hands because it has none. At the end, you open your hand and let go.
- **Rest.** The Company promises retirement and never pays it. Hal is always retiring on Friday. Renunciation ends in rest.
- **Hidden names.** H. COLOM is one. The catalysts are another (a proposal; the numbers pass decides which materials are catalysts): they sound like Motherload ores and decode to Latin anatomy. Cicatrium (layer 2, scar), Mycium (3, fungus), Lymphium (4), Nervium (5), Haemium (6, blood), Ossium (7, bone), Cordium (8, heart).
- **Echoes for writers, never quoted in game:**
  - Scripture: Psalm 115:4–8 (hands that handle not; those who make idols become like them), Isaiah 44:9–20 (the idol-maker's half-log), 1 Kings 18:27 (mocking the idol), Job 41 ("out of his mouth go burning lamps"), Ecclesiastes 5:10 (silver never satisfies), Matthew 25:1–13 (the lamps), Luke 14:33 (forsake all), Matthew 11:28 (rest).
  - Tolkien: Moria ("too greedily and too deep"), Gandalf's nameless things gnawing the world, Isengard's furnaces, the Dead Marshes, the Ringwraiths.
  - Lewis: the *Screwtape* preface's well-lit offices, and "not safe, but good."

## 7. Timeline

Dates are Colony Years (CY). Now is CY 61.

- **Before memory:** the Sleeper sleeps. Moloch fastens on and burrows toward the heart.
- **Long before the colony:** the Company. Chairmen come and go, every one a man who signed.
- **CY 0:** Survey One, a Behemoth under Commander Augustin Fell, sinks the Conduit through seven layers to the Furnace. Fell goes ahead alone "to interview for a position" and signs. Prosperity is founded the same year.
- **CY 0–19, the Boom:** 400 miners work layers 1–2. Death is an inconvenience: the Continuity Office rebuilds you. Veterans rebuilt too many times go quiet and stop using their lamps. Personnel's leaflets call them "Long-Term Staff"; the Continuity Office's records mark them "retained", and the miners took that word: the Retained.
- **CY 1:** Hal Brennan starts on the Claim.
- **CY 3:** Roz Achterberg arrives.
- **CY 19:** Ines and Tomas Kell take the Prospector below the Workings and drill a shaft into the Hollows. Tomas comes back without his lamp.
- **CY 20:** the Badger's three-man crew quits and walks down the Kells' shaft into the Hollows. Later that year, Ines leaves the Prospector at the end of the Workings' rail line, lights on for Tomas, and walks down the same shaft after him. None of them comes back.
- **CY 22:** shift steward Roz Achterberg follows the Conduit and finds that it goes *down*. She organises a walkout.
- **CY 23, Friday, the Night Shift,** the night before the walkout. It is Hal's last day; his send-off is booked at the Lamp & Pick. At 23:00 the Company calls all hands to Head Office at triple pay.
  - The Retained walk down.
  - The living go in nine convoys. Convoy Nine drowns in layer 4.
  - Hal takes his Mole down after Convoy Nine, which his wife, Mags Okoro, is leading. His pod goes dark at Black Water, and he is rebuilt into the dark.
  - Joy in Personnel had spent the week recording templates, because the Chairman told her she would be "unavailable" after Friday.
  - Roz refuses the summons. Alone in the emptied colony, she annotates every handbook in the stockroom for whoever comes next, then follows the convoys down in her Crawler.
  - By morning Prosperity is empty, and every terminal is dark except the contract terminal.
- **CY 23–61:** silence. The chapel candle keeps burning.
- **CY 61 (now):** Fell is nearly spent, and the Company reopens recruiting.

## 8. Prosperity

- **The town:** 400 bunks, all made up. The pay office, with stacks of uncollected pay stubs. The company store. The Lamp & Pick, the miners' bar, burned out; beside it only the footings of the old bandstand remain. Personnel's office, with Joy's desk.
- **Terminals:** fuel, ore processor, upgrades and the rest come back one by one as the charters repair them. The contract terminal never went dark: the only machine in town that never broke is the one that signs you up. (This is why a new charter can sign and get its handbook before anything is repaired.)
- **The Conduit** starts at the ore processor. It is an undiggable pipe stencilled COMPANY CONDUIT — DO NOT BREACH, at the same coordinates in every layer. It is a landmark through the whole game, and it ends in the Furnace.
- **The Founder statue** stands in the square (section 4).
- **The Continuity Office** is the colony's respawn point. *"Accidents happen! The Continuity Plan returns you to work at no cost to you."*
- **The chapel** is small, with no denomination and one candle. It is never explained.
- **The hangar** holds the founding Mole, MOLE-0001.
- **The records board** (proposal): a *Continuity Events* column (deaths), and after the finale, each charter's entry shows a lamp (renounced) or a bronze mask (Chairman).

## 9. Cast

- **Joy, Personnel** (acts 1–2). Every cheerful message and every bonus is one of her pre-recorded templates, with the system filling in your name. In act 2 the templates misfire. In the Lattice you hear **Template 0**, the one she recorded for herself. She is gone; her voice greets you at Head Office anyway.
- **H. Colom, the Chairman** (act 3 and the finale). Augustin Fell under the mask. Victorian gravitas, treats you as his property, ridiculous underneath. His corporate words curdle as he talks: *"…consumed. Concluded."*
- **Hal Brennan, MOLE-0117.** A friendly old hand who gives tips and retires on Friday after "twenty-two years." His last Friday was the Night Shift, when he went down after his wife's convoy. He is Retained, still on the radio under his pod's call sign though the pod lies in layer 4, and he doesn't know. In layer 4 you find his Mole, its last log entry dated that Friday.
- **Ines and Tomas Kell, the Prospector.** Ines's relay begs Tomas to turn his lamp on; her last Note is in the Hollows: *"It's quiet here. I'm going to stay a little."* The lampless figure on layer 2's rail line might be Tomas. The game never confirms it.
- **The Badger's crew.** Abe Pell, Sonny Orrin and Kit Dace, three drillers who quit in CY 20 and walked down into the Hollows. Their wreck has grown into a shrine.
- **Convoy Nine.** The drowned procession in layer 4, led by Captain Mags Okoro, Hal's wife, in HAULER-0009. Their beacons loop an original miners' hymn, their lamps are still lit under the water, and their faces through the glass are peaceful. *"Water's warm. Doesn't feel like drowning. Feels like being held."*
- **Roz Achterberg, "—R."** Shift steward, organiser of the walkout, the one who refused the Night Shift. Her margin notes are in every handbook. Her Notes run from layer 3 to the Furnace; her Crawler is a wreck at layer 6's Checkpoint; she sends act-3 transmissions as "Source unknown." In the Furnace, hers is the one Retained lamp still lit. Her thesis, which is the game's: *"He can't take what you won't give him."*
- **Benny.** A miner who went ahead without his lamp on the Night Shift. Hal still thinks Benny is buying at his send-off. Mentioned, never met.
- **Survey One.** The Behemoth wreck in layer 7. It proves the Company always knew, holds Fell's last log as a man, and carries the homage to the original's "motherload" moment.
- **The Lattice.** It replays the dead and says your crew's names in Hal's voice. It is never explained.

## 10. The three channels

- **Transmissions** carry the story: [lore/transmissions.md](lore/transmissions.md), which owns the triggers. Personnel pays bonuses in acts 1–2; in act 3 the Chairman sends receipts.
- **Relays** are transmissions from wrecks' beacons, repeating a dead crew's last words once a day since they died. The loop counter is part of the horror.
- **The handbook** carries the rules through layer 2, then falls silent. Its **revisions** change the tone; Roz's **margin notes** argue with it. See [lore/handbook.md](lore/handbook.md).
- **Notes** are the found record: wreck logs, torn pages, time cards, a hymn sheet. See [lore/notes.md](lore/notes.md). (The Chairmen's Book and the Ledger are handbook pages, not Notes.)
- **Work orders** carry small story beats (proposal): "Restore the Founder's hands" (Bronzium) in act 1; Personnel's "Morale Initiative" in act 2. They never stop paying.

## 11. Layers

Altimeter tricks are presentation only; the real depth is always what the game uses.

### 1 · The Claim (skin)

Zones: **Topsoil Claims → Stone Benches → Deep Claim.**

- Hand-dug first-generation shafts, lunch pails, candle niches with stubs and prayer cards. The Conduit's casing runs down beside the colony.
- Personnel pays milestone bonuses. Hal's first transmission: *"Somebody on the line."*
- Near the floor, a two-second fragment: *"…don't look at the walls when it goes quiet…"*
- Story beats: the bootstrap, the colony's first repairs, a world that feels merely abandoned.

### 2 · The Old Workings (scar tissue)

Zones: **Upper Levels → Shift Change → Prospector's Run.**

- **Upper Levels:** collapsed galleries, timber props, ore carts loaded and never hauled.
- **Shift Change:** the colony's underground rail hub. A punch clock still ticking; 400 time cards: all but one punched IN at 23:00 on the Friday, none OUT. The unpunched card is Roz's.
- **Prospector's Run:** the rail line to the breach, the Prospector wreck with one light still on, and **something moving in the dark**: a lampless figure walking the rails. It never attacks. It never stops.
- Relays with loop counters. Personnel: *"Crosstalk is a known fault on the Company side. No action is required!"*
- The handbook ends here: *"Further documentation is restricted to Senior Personnel,"* and Roz's margin: *"Nobody's ever been promoted."*
- Altimeter: the digits flicker now and then.
- Catalyst (proposal): Cicatrium, to restore the Prospector.

### 3 · Fungal Hollows (what grows on it)

Zones: **the Garden → Spore Fields → the Quiet.**

- **The Garden:** the miners' name for the first natural light. Beautiful, and the first real on-foot exploration.
- **Spore Fields:** fungus overgrowing abandoned Company equipment.
- **The Quiet:** a cathedral cavern where sound dies. The Badger wreck has grown into a shrine, surrounded by kneeling shapes. A transmission arrives here with nothing in it.
- Act 2 opens with handbook Rev. 2. Personnel's templates start to misfire. Hal talks about places in the colony that no longer exist.
- Ines's last Note. Catalyst (proposal): Mycium.

### 4 · The Drowned Deep (fluid)

Zones: **the Seep → the Drowned Road → Black Water → the Basin.**

- **The Drowned Road:** Convoy Nine lies in a line like a funeral procession, hatches sealed and lamps lit, HAULER-0009 at its head. The hymn plays on the sonar.
- **Black Water:** pressure, dark, and Hal's Mole. Its log ends on the Friday of the Night Shift. Hal has been dead for 38 years.
- **The Basin:** the breach is underwater.
- Personnel: *"Pressure readings are within acceptable limits!"* and *"Company altimeters are certified to Layer 5."*
- Catalyst (proposal): Lymphium.

### 5 · The Lattice (nerve)

Zones: **Geode Fields → Singing Halls → the Switchboard.**

- **Singing Halls:** drilling rings the crystal, and things come to the sound. Every Company transmission echoes with its sender reversed. The Lattice replays fragments of every voice ever heard down here.
- **The Switchboard:** crystal threads carry pulses of light downward. Ghosts on the scanner. Your crew's names, spoken in Hal's voice.
- Altimeter: shows the depth twice, the second time with its digits reversed.
- **Template 0:** Joy's own warning. *"Whatever he offers you at the bottom, don't sign it. Keep your lamp lit."*
- At the floor: *"Your account has been escalated. The Chairman will handle your charter personally."*
- Catalyst (proposal): Nervium.

### 6 · The Magma Belt (blood)

Zones: **Cinder Crust → the Arteries → Checkpoint.**

- **The Arteries:** lava rivers pulse downward on a slow beat, about twelve to the minute, tapped by Company siphon pipes.
- **Checkpoint:** a gate in the rock, booths, and a sign asking HAVE YOU BEEN AUTHORISED? The recording refuses you and lets you through anyway. Roz's Crawler is here; her log says she went on alone, on foot.
- The Chairman's first messages: polite, then "turn back," always with a receipt. Handbook Rev. 3.
- A lampless figure carrying a radio that crackles on your channel.
- Altimeter: the label changes to DISTANCE FROM AUTHORISED AREA.
- Catalyst (proposal): Haemium.

### 7 · The Ossuary (bone)

Zones: **Rib Vaults → Old Furnaces → the Watching Wall → Floor of Bones.**

- **Rib Vaults:** curved bones the size of cathedrals. Survey One's survey sheets recommend that staff stop calling them ribs.
- **Old Furnaces:** Moloch's cold feeding sites, each built around an open-handed shape, and a heap of bronze masks: every Chairman before Fell. Roz: *"Count the masks."*
- **The Watching Wall:** **THE EYES.** They open in the bone when your lights go off. A Survey-era placard reads *"Personnel observing eyes are to continue working. Eyes are not a hazard"*: the Company's one accidentally true statement. Survey One's relay: *"THEY'RE OPEN. ALL OF THEM."* The Chairman: *"There are no eyes in the Ossuary."*
- **Floor of Bones:** Survey One's Behemoth. Fell's last log as a man: *"He says there's a position open. I'm going down to interview."* The last relay: *"Sir, the floor is warm. Sir, it's beating. This is the motherload."*
- Altimeter: while the eyes are open, the digits are replaced by ◉.
- Catalyst (proposal): Ossium.

### 8 · The Furnace (the heart cavity)

Zones: **Intake → the Works → the Boardroom.** The Ramp lies below.

- **Intake:** the Conduit pours ore into the fire. The Retained work the lines under a sign: *Smile! You're on Company premises.* Hal is among them, with his radio.
- **The Works:** assembly lines where every chassis you ever salvaged was built, and the bronze figure at the centre of Head Office, nearly finished. Roz works the third line from the fire, her lamp still lit.
- **The Boardroom:** a long table at the edge of the fire.
- Joy's last template welcomes you to Head Office. Handbook Rev. 4.
- Altimeter: YOU ARE ON COMPANY PREMISES.
- Catalyst (proposal): Cordium, for post-game tiers.

## 12. The finale

Combat details belong to the creatures session ([#13](https://github.com/pkeppeler/deepcharter/issues/13)); this is the story's shape.

1. **Phase 1, the Chairman.** Fell in a frock coat and the smiling bronze mask, hands always open. The sender field's letters slide into MOLOCH. He is pompous and ridiculous, and mocking him is the right response.
2. **Phase 2, Head Office stands up.** The bronze figure rises, built from what *this charter* sold, so each charter fights its own. You break it with drill and charge, but you cannot kill it: everything it is made of was given to it.
3. **The offer.** The mask crawls out of the wreck, small and hollow, with Moloch speaking through it: *"…Every notice said turn back. You came anyway. That is what we look for in a Chairman. The position is yours. Take it. All of it."* The **Controlling Interest** drops: a physical deed. The wreck of Head Office, the richest haul in the game, is part of what is offered.
4. **Signing** (the Director only, at the Boardroom table). Final and permanent.
   - The Director becomes the Chairman. The deed binds to him, impossible to lose, and his suit lamp goes dark for good. Both follow him even if he leaves the charter.
   - The wreck's wealth and a recurring dividend are credited, from the labour of the Retained, who never rest. Every dividend notice asks for more.
   - The Furnace relights. The Retained turn to face their new Chairman. Moloch wears a new mask.
   - For that charter, the altimeter reads YOU ARE ON COMPANY PREMISES everywhere, including the surface.
   - Last exchange: Hal asks, *"Is it Friday?"* The Company answers, *"No."*
5. **Renunciation** (anyone in the charter holding the deed throws it into the Furnace). The true ending, and the only thing that kills him.
   - An idol given nothing cannot live. The mask burns in its own fire, and the wreck of Head Office slumps into it.
   - Every coin in the charter's account goes with it. Serial numbers burn off every pod and component: the parts read UNNUMBERED, and they are yours. (Binding to the charter is unchanged; the rules never change.)
   - The Retained lay down their tools and sit down where they stand, one by one: rest. Hal: *"Is it Friday? Feels like Friday."* His channel closes after 38 years.
   - Silence. Then one heartbeat that knocks everyone flat.
   - The Boardroom floor cracks. Beneath it an eye the size of the arena opens and looks at you. It closes. No transmission covers this moment.
   - Light rises from the crack, and every crew member receives an **Unnumbered Lamp**: no serial number, nobody's property, and it never goes out. It belongs to the player, not the charter.
   - Roz's lamp alone does not go out. It walks down toward the Ramp.

**Deed rules** (design intent): only members of the charter that broke Head Office can hold its deed. The deed cannot leave the Boardroom; if its holder dies or leaves, it returns to the table. Nothing ends until someone signs or burns it.

## 13. Post-game

- **Both paths:** the Ramp, then UNCHARTED. The strata repeat the layers above, warped. Nameless things gnaw there: older than Moloch, with no lures and no Companies. They just eat. The records board tracks how deep each charter gets.
- **If renounced:**
  - The terminals belong to the colony and pay the same prices. Personnel's voice is gone.
  - The Continuity Office still works, and nobody knows who runs it now.
  - Rare "—R." Notes appear in the uncharted layers: Roz is ahead of you.
  - You go down because something is eating the thing that held the drowned.
- **If signed:** quota notices drive the Chairman ever deeper. The dividend never satisfies.
- **Splice event:** when a new story layer is spliced in between the Furnace and the Ramp, the depths move. It stirred.

## 14. Hooks for the creatures session

Three families:

- **Moloch's:** the Retained and Company machines (siphon crawlers, checkpoint wardens).
- **The Sleeper's defences:** things that treat drills as an infection.
- **The gnawers:** post-game only.

Fixed beats: something moving in the dark (layer 2, never attacks), listeners drawn to resonance (layer 5), THE EYES that never attack (layer 7), and the two-phase finale (layer 8).
