# The Employee Handbook: text and revisions

Settled 2026-10-07. Canon and context: [LORE.md](../LORE.md); the handbook's rules and scope: [SPEC.md section 5](../SPEC.md#5-employee-handbook-and-teaching-the-rules). Text inside quote blocks is **player-facing**.

## How the handbook speaks

- **The Company's voice:** cheerful, second person, short sentences, an exclamation mark per paragraph at most. Screwtape's office manners: never raises its voice, always has a policy.
- **Euphemisms are the joke:** "relocated", "retained", "Continuity Event", "unavailable", "Long-Term Staff". Never explain them.
- **The rules are always true and explicit.** The Company lies about what the deep *is*, never about how the game *works*. A revision may change tone, add clauses or stamp a page; it never changes a rule. (Guardrail 3 in [LORE.md](../LORE.md#guardrails).)
- **Margin notes** are Roz's handwriting, signed "—R.", the same in every copy. She wrote them alone on the night of the Night Shift (CY 23), so they know only what was true that night. The Company can rewrite its printed text remotely; it can't touch her ink.
- **Directives** are imperative, one action each, and complete automatically. The lists below are drafts; the implementation owns the final wording and triggers.
- **Visibility** follows the spec: the current chapter in full, the next chapter's title and Directives, everything further CLASSIFIED.
- **Spec sheets** (the first time something is bought or crafted) use plain technical language on Company letterhead and carry no story, for example: *"PAD, PAIRED. Power cost rises with depth and mass. Carries players and pods with empty cargo bays. Mined materials cannot be transported."*

## The chapters

### 1. Welcome to Prosperity (bootstrap crafting)

> Prosperity has been a little quiet lately, so you'll start the way our founders did: with your own two hands. Gather wood and stone, make yourself some tools, and dig up the iron and copper you'll need for repairs. Everything you craft is yours to keep. Nothing builds character like a fresh start!
>
> *Directives (draft):* Gather wood. · Craft a crafting table. · Craft stone tools. · Mine iron ore. · Smelt iron.
>
> *Margin:* Every terminal in town went dark tonight except the contract terminal. Think about that. —R.

### 2. Bringing Prosperity Back Online (repairing the terminals)

> Prosperity's terminals were taken offline after the Night Shift, and we need you to bring them back, one at a time. Each terminal shows the parts it needs. Start with the fuel pump, then the ore processor, then the upgrade terminal. When any charter repairs a terminal, it works for everyone. That's the Prosperity spirit!
>
> Prosperity's previous workforce has been relocated. We are confident you will make the colony your own.
>
> *Directives (draft):* Repair the fuel pump. · Repair the ore processor. · Repair the upgrade terminal.
>
> *Margin, beside the second paragraph:* They change this page. They always change this page. —R.
>
> *Margin, beside the ore processor:* Put your ear to the processor. Refineries don't sound like that. —R.

### 3. Meet the Mole (repairing the founding pod)

> In the hangar you'll find the Mole, the first pod Prosperity ever put to work. She needs a little love before she flies again. Each repair the hangar lists brings her closer. (If another charter has already repaired her, a refurbished Mole is available at the hangar for a modest fee.)
>
> *Directives (draft):* Repair the Mole. · Board the Mole.
>
> *Margin:* MOLE-0001. She's carried more of us than the bunkhouse has. Be good to her. —R.

### 4. Fuel Is Life (fuel, driving, flying and drilling)

> Fuel is the lifeblood of every Colom pod! Your Mole burns fuel whenever she is powered on, faster while moving and fastest while drilling. Drive on her treads, fly with her rotor, and drill down or sideways, but never up. Watch the gauge, listen for the low-fuel tone, and always plan your trip home. A pod that runs dry is **Stranded**: she powers down and goes dark until someone rescues her or she's refuelled from a reserve tank.
>
> *Directives (draft):* Refuel the Mole at the colony pump. · Fly the Mole. · Drill down ten blocks. · Return to the colony.
>
> *Margin:* Don't let her go dark in the Workings. —R.

### 5. Every Sale Counts (selling and upgrading)

> Ore is heavy, so the Mole is your hauler. Bring ore to the ore processor to sell it; every sale is credited to your charter's account. Spend your earnings at the upgrade terminal on better parts. All parts are licensed to your charter and carry a Company serial number. Ore gets richer the farther down you go!
>
> *Directives (draft):* Sell ore at the ore processor. · Buy a component at the upgrade terminal. · Install it in the Mole.
>
> *Margin:* Ask where it goes after. The Conduit only runs one way. —R.

### 6. Seeing Below (the scanner)

> Your scanner shows a slice of the ground around your pod, so you can find ore before you dig for it. Better scanners see farther and show more. Hazards that can't be seen with the eye will show on higher-tier scanners.
>
> *Directives (draft):* Install a scanner. · Find ore with the scanner.
>
> *Margin:* If a shape on the scanner moves, it isn't ore. —R.

### 7. Staying Safe on the Claim (layer 1's hazards)

> The Claim is the friendliest ground we have, but it isn't harmless. Watch for undiggable rock, lava you can see, gas pockets you can't, and hard landings. Your hull takes the damage, so repair it at the colony. Accidents happen! The Continuity Plan returns you to work at no cost to you.
>
> *Directives (draft):* Repair your hull at the colony. · Reach the Deep Claim.
>
> *Margin:* Not listed: what's in the Workings. —R.

### 8. Your First Breach

> At the floor of the Claim you'll meet a breach: a hard crust between layers. Any drill can get through, slowly, and it's hard on the hull and the heat. Top up your fuel and repair your hull first. On the other side are the Old Workings, Prosperity's original mine levels and a proud part of our heritage.
>
> *Directives (draft):* Breach into the Old Workings.
>
> *Margin:* Every breach is a door. We didn't put the doors there. —R.

### 9. Recovering Company Property (the Prospector)

> Somewhere in the Old Workings lies the Prospector, a two-seat pod that went missing some years ago. Find her, tow her home and restore her, and she's yours to fly. She has a navigator's seat, so bring a friend! Then take her down to the floor of the Workings. You've earned it.
>
> *Directives (draft):* Find the Prospector. · Tow the Prospector to the colony. · Restore the Prospector. · Reach the floor of the Old Workings.
>
> *Margin:* Ines's pod. Read her log, then decide if you still want the job. —R.

### The end of the official chapters

> Further documentation is restricted to Senior Personnel.
>
> *Margin:* Nobody's ever been promoted. —R.

## Revisions

Each revision arrives with a notice at the top of the handbook (*"Your Employee Handbook has been updated (Rev. N). Changes are marked ▸."*) and a mark beside each changed passage. Rules text never changes.
