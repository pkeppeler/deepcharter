# Evidence and before/after stills

- `test/evidence/` holds the PR evidence scenarios (see `tools/record-evidence.sh`). `tools/record-evidence.sh <scenario>` runs only that scenario's class (`-PclientTests`), found by scanning for the `EvidenceScenario` whose `name()` returns the id, so a recording takes the scenario's own time plus client start-up; an unknown id fails before a client starts. `--full-suite` runs the whole client suite instead, `--print-class` prints the class. When the Mac cannot render, add the `record` label to a PR whose body has a `Record: <scenario>` line (or run the `Record evidence` workflow with `pr` and `scenario`): CI runs the scenario headless, publishes its stills, GIF and MP4 to `pr-media/<pr>/`, and comments the markdown. Costs CI minutes, so only on request.
- A scenario never stages at a fixed world position: derive the place from the colony's anchors (`Colony.anchor`, `ColonySite`), a `RoomCarver` room, the layer's own geometry (`LayerTuning.DEFAULT.crustThickness()`) or `EvidenceScenario.LIVE_GAUGES_FLOOR_Y`, `LAYER_ROOM_Y` and `SKY_SLAB_Y`; a Y near a layer floor records the signal-decay blackout. `checkFixedPosGate` fails a literal world Y (a `*_Y` constant, a Y in `teleportTo`, `setPos`, `moveTo`, `atY`, a Y in a `BlockPos` or `Vec3` beside literal X and Z of 100 or more) unless it carries `// fixed-pos: <reason>`. It does not flag a lowercase or local Y, a Y set after its declaration, `.above(n)` from a run-time position, a Y beside a variable X or Z, or small all-literal offsets: check those by eye.
- The README tour is refreshed with `tools/readme-tour.sh [item...]` (manifest `docs/readme-tour.tsv`). Run it after a PR that changes something the tour shows, and after an art PR. See [docs/tooling/readme-tour.md](../../../docs/tooling/readme-tour.md).
- Before and after stills for an art PR (`design-tour`, the same views of the whole game):
  1. Record before on `main`, from a checkout or worktree of `main` (never switch the main checkout): `tools/record-evidence.sh design-tour`. It takes about 30 minutes and ends in `build/evidence/design-tour/screenshots`. Copy that directory away, because the next run replaces it.
  2. Record after on the PR branch the same way, and copy its `screenshots` directory away too.
  3. Compare: `tools/diff-stills <before> <after> --design-tour --out <dir>`.
  4. Also run it without `--design-tour` (`tools/diff-stills <before> <after> --out <dir2>`) and look at every still the PR touches.
  5. Attach the table and the side-by-side image of each still the PR meant to change. Expect a few false positives (see below): open the `--out` images of each still the PR did not mean to change before you call it a finding.

  `tools/diff-stills` reports every still as a size mismatch against a tour recorded before #285 (800 x 450), so before and after must both come from after #285.

  `tools/diff-stills <dirA> <dirB> [--tolerance N] [--noise PREFIX=TOL:PERCENT] [--design-tour] [--out DIR]` needs only `python3`. It prints a table of the stills that changed (name, percent of pixels changed, largest channel delta; channels within `--tolerance` of each other, default 2, count as equal), and writes `NAME-side-by-side.png` and `NAME-diff.png` for each of them into `DIR`. It exits 1 when any still shows in the table. `--design-tour` applies `NOISE_FAMILIES` (in the tool): a family of stills that never comes out the same twice gets its own tolerance and a share of pixels under which it is hidden. Every hidden still is listed after the table ("N stills hidden by floors") with its numbers at the plain tolerance, and the summary says "floors applied". `--design-tour` with an explicit `--tolerance` is an error.

  The tour holds still what it can. Three runs of one commit differ in 0 stills with `--design-tour` (all three pairs; #435 measured it), and in 0 or 1 without it (`colony-aerial-northwest`), which `--design-tour` hides. Before #309 two runs of one commit differed in 78 of the 151 stills at the plain tolerance, and in 19 with the floors of the time. The two stills that can still differ are the `NOISE_FAMILIES` entries: `colony-aerial-northwest` (terrain at the far edge of the loaded chunks: 1.86% of the pixels, up to 60 levels) and `screen-pod-cargo` (four pixels of one row, up to 5 levels). `screen-pod-cargo` stays a floor until a longer sample shows it is not needed. The cause of neither is found.

  `DesignTourScenario` fixes the seed, stops the clock at noon and the weather clear, stops random ticks and mob spawning, clears mobs and particles before each still, parks the cursor off the window, and waits (on a wall-clock limit, not on tick counts) until the chunks have rendered, the light has settled and the camera and the pod it rides have stopped moving. It also holds still, through switches in `test/support/EvidencePins` that mixins of the gametest mod read (`test/mixin`, never shipped; a Minecraft update that renames a target stops the client run at launch):
  - animated textures (lava) on their first frame; no block light flicker (vanilla moves the brightness of every block-lit surface by a random walk on each client tick: lamps, candles, a pod's lights);
  - no HUD vignette (its brightness eases over ticks), no held item name (it fades on ticks) and no hands (the rider's arm);
  - every entity but a pod at rest (no idle sway or walk cycle: lampless figures, the player in the inventory screen), the lampless figure of each still facing south (a new entity gets a random yaw), the figure by the lit pod posed beside the pod, whole, and no figures spawned on the rails;
  - the animation clock of every lampless figure held at age 0: GeckoLib animates a figure on its own clock (the entity's ticks plus the frame's partial tick), which the vanilla `ageInTicks` pin above does not reach, so each still shows the first frame of the figure's idle animation. Pods are GeckoLib too and keep their clock, because the tour shows them animated and they did not differ between runs;
  - the breach fade and the typing of the transmission held at half black from the moment the crossing begins, until the still is taken;
  - the tour's charter founded with a fixed id (`Charters.found(server, founder, name, id)`), because a pod's paint is the slot of the id's hash (ADR 0041) and a random id paints it a new colour in each run;
  - the carved rooms (the dark room, the layer-1 crust room, the layer-2 hall): a client that is sent a chunk while the light engine still works on a room carved in it keeps the old light (sky light 15 in a sealed room), and vanilla sends no change that clears it, so some runs lit the room (issue 427). `RoomCarver.carve` and the tour's own dark-room carve call `LightSync.holdSendUntilLit` after the last block, so the chunk is sent only after its light is computed. Settle compares the light the client reads in each carved room it holds with the server's (`test/support/ClientLight`) and names the room if they differ at its deadline; it sends nothing.

  What a floor can hide (blind spots), at 854 x 480 = 409,920 pixels (the default window at GUI scale 2, a 427 x 240 GUI, which is also the size of every evidence frame):
  - `colony-aerial-northwest` (tolerance 2, 2%): a change of up to 8,198 pixels in that one still.
  - `screen-pod-cargo` (tolerance 5, no share): a change of under 6 levels anywhere in that one still.
  - Every other still has no floor: any channel change over 2 levels shows (a change of 1 or 2 levels anywhere is hidden by the plain tolerance).

  Run step 4 as well: a still the PR did not mean to change but that shows in the table is a finding, because no floor stands in front of it. If a new still differs between two runs of one commit, fix it in `DesignTourScenario.settle` or `pinTheClient` (a switch in `EvidencePins`) where you can, and add its prefix to `NOISE_FAMILIES` where you cannot, with the numbers you measured.

## Concept rounds

A concept round shows each option at least once in its real in-game place and lighting (fog, grade, lamp light), not only in a comparison set: a staged backdrop can flatter an option in a way the game never shows it.

A rock texture round (`layer-concepts`) is judged on `tools/layer_concepts/grid.py` over the `wide-wall` and `vanilla-reference` stills, which `tools/record-evidence.sh` runs, and on the eye, not on the seam check alone: the seam check reads textures and cannot see a grid that a model or a motif makes, and grid.py does not see a point motif.
